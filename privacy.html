Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Pushtimarg Nitya Path ("the app") respects your privacy. In short: we do not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not require an account, login, or any personal details, and has no advertising or analytics.

Data stored on your device

The app saves simple preferences (such as favourites or settings) only on your own device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Internet and third parties

All paths and texts are bundled inside the app and work offline. The app does not send data to any server. If the app opens an external link (for example in your browser), that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
